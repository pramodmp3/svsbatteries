About SVS Batteries

Your most trusted partner for high-quality battery sales and expert maintenance services since 2010. Powering your world with reliability and excellence.

Our History

Founded in 2010, SVS Batteries began with a simple mission: to provide the community with dependable power solutions. What started as a small service center has grown into a leading retailer and service provider in the region.


Over the last decade, we have navigated the evolving landscape of battery technology—from lead-acid to lithium-ion—ensuring our customers always receive the most advanced and efficient products. Our growth is built on a foundation of transparency, technical expertise, and an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To be the premier provider of reliable, affordable, and sustainable battery solutions that empower homes and businesses while minimizing environmental impact.

Our Mission

To exceed customer expectations through quality products, expert technical support, and a "service-first" approach that ensures long-term power security for every client.
